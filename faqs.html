Frequently Asked Questions

1. What services does RoomNest provide?

Travell offers a wide range of travel services including flight bookings, hotel accommodations, transportation arrangements, tour packages, travel insurance, and more.

2. How do I book a trip with RoomNest?

Booking a trip with Travell is easy. You can visit our website, use our mobile app, or contact our customer service to make reservations for flights, hotels, tours, and other travel-related services.

3. Can I customize my travel itinerary?

Yes, Travell offers customizable travel itineraries. You can choose destinations, activities, and accommodations according to your preferences, and our experts will help tailor the perfect trip for you.

4. What if I need to cancel or change my travel plans?

Travell has a cancellation and modification policy. The terms vary depending on the type of booking, so it's best to refer to the specific booking details or contact our customer service for assistance.

5. Is travel insurance available?

Yes, Travell provides travel insurance options to ensure you have coverage for unexpected events such as trip cancellations, medical emergencies, lost luggage, and more.

6. Are group discounts available?

Travell offers group travel packages and discounts for larger parties. Contact our group booking department for more information on group rates and benefits.

7. How do I receive my travel documents?

Your travel documents, such as e-tickets and hotel vouchers, will be sent to you electronically via email or can be accessed through our mobile app.

8. What languages does Travell's customer support speak?

Travell's customer support team is fluent in multiple languages to assist travelers from around the world.

9. Are there any loyalty or rewards programs for frequent travelers?

Yes, Travell has a loyalty program that rewards frequent travelers with points, discounts, and exclusive offers.

10. Are there any travel restrictions or entry requirements I should be aware of?

Travell stays updated on travel advisories, visa requirements, and health and safety regulations for different destinations. However, it's always recommended to check with relevant authorities before your trip.

11. What payment methods are accepted?

Travell accepts a variety of payment methods including credit/debit cards, bank transfers, and mobile payment options.
